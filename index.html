FAQs

Questions, answered

Still planning? Call us on +92 323 7703373.

Does it cost anything?

No. The evening, the dinner and the draw cost nothing, and nothing is for sale.

What exactly do I win?

A scholarship paying 100% of the tuition fees for one Master's (postgraduate taught) course at a Study Planners partner university in the UK, with no upper limit. Living costs, accommodation, visa fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge and travel are not included, and the university's entry requirements still apply.

Can I win if I can't come?

No. The winner is drawn from guests checked in at the venue and must be there when their name is called.

What is the deadline for seat requests?

23:59 on the day before your evening: Wednesday 28 October for London, Sunday 1 November for Birmingham and Wednesday 4 November for Manchester. Seats are limited, so a city can close sooner.

Which ID is accepted?

Your original passport or UK photo driving licence, in the same name as your request. Copies and phone photos are not accepted.

How do I know I have a seat?

After review, we send confirmation by email or SMS ahead of the evening. Please check your spam folder too.

Can someone come with me?

Seats are individual, so anyone joining you needs to send their own request here.

Terms

Scholarship draw terms

The key points: free to enter; one 100% tuition fee scholarship per event; register online by 23:59 the day before your event; attend in person with original photo ID; winner drawn at random on the night.

Read the full terms and conditions
  1. Promoter. Study Planners.
  2. Free entry. The draw is free to enter. No purchase or payment is needed to enter or to win.
  3. Who can enter. UK residents aged 18 or over who hold valid UK immigration permission or settled status. Employees of Study Planners, its group companies and their immediate families cannot enter.
  4. How to enter. (a) Send a seat request through this page by 23:59 (UK time) on the day before your chosen event; (b) receive an approved ticket from Study Planners; (c) attend the event in person and check in with an original passport or UK photo driving licence matching the name on your request. One entry per person, for one event only.
  5. Events and closing dates. London, Thursday 29 October 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 28 October). Birmingham, Monday 2 November 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 1 November). Manchester, Thursday 5 November 2026 (requests close 23:59 on 4 November). Doors open at 17:00 and dinner starts at 18:00.
  6. Seats. Seats at each venue are limited and are given at the Promoter's discretion after reviewing each request. Not every request will receive a ticket.
  7. The prize. One prize per event (three in total): a scholarship covering 100% of the tuition fees, with no upper limit, for one postgraduate taught (Master's) course at a UK partner university of Study Planners. The scholarship is valid only for one-year (full-time) Master's courses at BPP University and cannot be applied to any other university or course length.
  8. What the prize does not cover. Living costs, accommodation, visa application fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge, travel, deposits or any other cost. No cash alternative is offered. The prize cannot be transferred.
  9. Conditions of the prize. The winner must meet the university's academic and English language entry requirements, receive and accept an offer, and meet any UK immigration requirements for the course.
  10. The draw. One winner per event is drawn at random, live at the event, from guests checked in with valid ID. The winner must be present when drawn; if not, another winner is drawn.
  11. Winner announcement. The winner is announced on the night. With the winner's consent, their first name and city may be published on Study Planners's website and social media.
  12. Your data. Personal details are used to manage the event and the draw under the Study Planners privacy policy [to confirm: privacy policy link (the website has none)]. Marketing messages are sent only if you opt in.
  13. Social media. This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or WhatsApp. By entering, you release these platforms from any liability connected with the promotion.
  14. Changes. The Promoter may change or cancel an event or the draw if circumstances outside its control require it, and will give notice where possible.
